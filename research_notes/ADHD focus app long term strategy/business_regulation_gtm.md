## Monetization: freemium vs paywall, pricing, regional (India PPP), B2B2C, reimbursement, benchmarks

### Takeaway
Health & Fitness is the best-monetizing subscription category, with the highest trial-to-paid conversion (about 35–38% median). Annual-plan cancellations are rising, and trials are getting shorter, which makes durable revenue harder. Hard paywalls convert about 6x better than freemium with similar retention. India needs PPP pricing plus UPI, at roughly ₹150–400 a month. Insurance or prescription reimbursement is a trap for a startup outside Germany.

### Inferences
- Lead with an annual plan and a 5–9 day trial, or a hard paywall after a valuable free first session. Treat rising annual churn (about 72% cancel in year 1) as the central business risk, and plan to earn the second renewal.
- Benchmark positioning: one sec is about $20/year, Tiimo about $54/year, Inflow $96–200/year. A wellness-positioned student focus app plausibly sits at $30–60/year in the US and about ₹99–199/month or about ₹799–1,499/year in India, with UPI Autopay. These are inferred from comparables, not tested.
- Lifetime deals (as one sec offers) suit indie cash flow but cap LTV. Use them sparingly, for example as a high-priced anchor or a launch promotion.
- In India, the payer is often the parent or the institution, so school and coaching-institute licences (B2B2C) also solve the DPDP minor-consent problem through the education exemption, subject to legal review.
- Do not build a US business that depends on insurance reimbursement. Germany's DiGA is the only proven ADHD reimbursement path, and it requires CE-marked medical device status plus evidence. Treat it as a year-3 to year-5 option, not a core plan.

## Retention: what drives long-term retention; ethical design vs. dark patterns; one sec, Forest, Headspace, Duolingo

### Takeaway
Durable retention comes from identity and habit mechanics (streaks, visible progress, shared goals) layered on real outcomes. Dark-pattern retention, such as hard-to-cancel subscriptions, now draws class actions and state auto-renewal law exposure even though the federal click-to-cancel rule was vacated.

### Cited Findings
- Duolingo: 52.7M DAUs in Q4 2025, up about 29–30% YoY. DAU/MAU was about 37% in Q2 2025 and has risen almost every quarter. More than 5M users have streaks of a year or longer, and more than half of daily learners have a 7+ day streak, double the share a year earlier. A one-time June event revived over 15M streaks. DAU growth decelerated through 2025, guidance is about 20% for 2026, and the goal is 100M DAU by 2028, accepting slower bookings growth to get there — [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/duolingo-reports-40-percent-dau-growth); [Motley Fool Q2 2025 transcript](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/08/06/duolingo-duol-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript); [PYMNTS 2026](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/duolingo-bets-on-user-growth-to-outpace-ai-disruption/); [Yahoo Q4 highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/duolingo-q4-earnings-call-highlights-041400802.html)
- one sec: a PNAS paper (Feb 2023, co-authored by the developer, so not independent) studied 280 people over 6 weeks. The friction pause led users to close the target app about 36% of the time, and open attempts fell about 37%. Marketing claims a 57% reduction, which is a different metric. Revenue was "doubling every single month" early on — [one sec research](https://one-sec.app/research/); [Haliburton et al. CHI 2024 longitudinal friction study](https://www.medien.ifi.lmu.de/pubdb/publications/pub/haliburton2024chi/haliburton2024chi.pdf); [RevenueCat interview](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/frederik-riedel-expected-12-his-app-cut-screen-time-by-57.md); [Time](https://tollbit.time.com/6240981/social-media-addiction-app/)
- Forest: gamified focus where a virtual tree grows during focus time, tied to real tree planting (more than 2M trees claimed). This is an ethical, purpose-linked reward loop — [Forest](https://forestapp.cc/)
- Freemium retention is roughly equal to paywall retention (27% vs 28%), so a free tier does not buy retention by itself — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/04/new-report-looks-into-the-sustainability-of-the-subscription-app-market-for-2026/)
- Dark patterns: Noom paid $62M ($56M cash and $6M credits) to settle the Mahood v. Noom class action. It was alleged to make cancellation hard by not allowing cancellation through email, phone, the website or by deleting the app. The settlement required a cancel button and separate auto-renewal opt-in — [Hunton](https://www.hunton.com/privacy-and-cybersecurity-law-blog/2022/02/22/fitness-app-agrees-to-pay-56-million-to-settle-class-action-alleging-dark-pattern-practices/); [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/noom-to-pay-over-60m-to-cancel-automatic-renewal-suit); [deceptive.design](https://www.deceptive.design/enforcement/geraldine-mahood-v-noom-inc)
- The FTC's October 2021 enforcement policy statement warned against dark patterns in subscription sign-ups. Vonage paid $100M (Nov 2022) over hard-to-cancel subscriptions — [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/perspectives-events/publications/2022/11/subscription-lessons-from-us-ftcs-latest-dark-patterns-settlement)
- The Eighth Circuit vacated the FTC click-to-cancel rule on July 8, 2025, on procedural grounds. ROSCA, the TSR and FTC Act Section 5 still apply, and more than half of US states have automatic-renewal laws, many as strict as the vacated rule — [DLA Piper](https://dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2025/07/ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule-voided); [WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/sitecore/content/whlaunch/home/research/news-publications/20250801-eighth-circuit-vacates-the-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule-but-federal-and-state-regulators-likely-to-remain-active); [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/7/eighth-circuit-vacates-ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule)
- Inflow's Trustpilot reviews include complaints about renewals and refunds ("took my money anyway"), a reputational risk for ADHD audiences prone to forgotten renewals — [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/getinflow.io?page=4)

### Inferences
- For ADHD users, forgotten renewals and "ADHD tax" anger are a predictable churn and review risk. Ethical defaults work as a brand moat: pre-renewal reminders, one-tap cancel, pause options, and forgiving streaks such as streak freezes or Duolingo-style repair events.
- Copy one sec's mechanism: evidence-backed friction plus publishing research builds trust and press. Copy Duolingo's: streaks and social accountability. Avoid shame-based notifications, which jar with an ADHD audience.
- Since annual renewals are the weak point, retention work should target months 9–12: progress reports, "year in focus" recaps and outcome evidence before renewal.

## Regulation: medical device thresholds (FDA, EU MDR, CDSCO), health data privacy (HIPAA, FTC HBNR, GDPR, DPDP, COPPA), Google Play declarations

### Takeaway
Claims decide everything. "Helps you focus and build study habits" is general wellness. "Treats, diagnoses or manages ADHD symptoms" is a medical device in the US, EU and India. HIPAA rarely applies to a D2C app, but the FTC (Section 5 plus the Health Breach Notification Rule) does. Under-18 users trigger COPPA (amended rule, compliance since April 22, 2026) and India's DPDP verifiable parental consent for under-18s (in force about May 2027).

### Cited Findings
**US FDA**
- On Jan 6, 2026, FDA issued revised final General Wellness and Clinical Decision Support guidances, superseding the 2019 and 2022 versions. The two-factor test is unchanged: the product is intended only for general wellness use and presents low risk. Whether a product qualifies depends on how it is advertised and promoted — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-it/insights/publications/2026/01/fda-updates-its-clinical-decision-support-and-general-wellness-guidances-key-points); [Faegre Drinker](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance); [Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/01/fda-adapts-with-the-times-on-digital-health-updated-guidances-on-general-wellness-products); [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/zh-hans/insights/2026/01/a-relaxing-2026-fda-updates-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance)
- The Policy for Device Software Functions and Mobile Medical Applications continues to apply — [Faegre Drinker](https://faegredrinker.com/en/insights/publications/2026/1/key-updates-in-fdas-2026-general-wellness-and-clinical-decision-support-software-guidance)
- EndeavorRx is a precedent: an ADHD "treatment" game required FDA authorization and a prescription — [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2023/09/13/akili-endeavorrx-prescription-business-model-lay-offs/)

**EU MDR**
- MDCG 2019-11 Rev.1 (17 June 2025) governs qualification and classification of software and adds wellness and lifestyle examples. Qualification hinges on intended purpose. Software is likely a medical device if it acts on data beyond storage, communication or simple search, for a medical purpose, for an individual patient. Under Rule 11, Class I is narrow and most diagnosis or therapy software is Class IIa or higher — [EC MDCG 2019-11 rev1](https://health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/update-mdcg-2019-11-rev1-qualification-and-classification-software-regulation-eu-2017745-and-2025-06-17_en); [Emergo](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/european-revision-primary-software-guidance-mdcg-2019-11-revision-1-small-changes-meaningful); [Johner Institute](https://blog.johner-institute.com/regulatory-affairs/mdr-rule-11/)
- Google Play updated its Health and Medical Functionalities policy to incorporate MDCG guidance for medical device apps targeting EU users (Oct 2025 preview) — [Google Play policy preview](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13996823)

**India CDSCO**
- CDSCO issued draft Medical Device Software guidance on Oct 21, 2025, and the final version (CDSCO/MD/GD/MDSW/01/2026) by circular on July 21, 2026. Only software with a medical purpose (diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, prediction, treatment) falls under the Medical Devices Rules 2017. SaMD class depends on intended use, under a four-tier risk system — [Nishith Desai](https://nishithdesai.com/research-and-articles/hotline/pharma-healthcare-update/the-next-frontier-in-medtech-regulation-dissecting-cdscos-draft-guidance-on-medical-device-software-15465); [Cyril Amarchand](https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2026/01/medical-device-as-software-has-cdsco-guidance-changed-the-rules/); [Emergo final guidance](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/india-cdsco-finalizes-guidance-medical-device-software?linkId=987899634); [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/cdsco-draft-guidance-medical-software-40691.html)

**Health data privacy, US**
- FTC Health Breach Notification Rule amendments (final April 26, 2024; effective July 29, 2024) explicitly cover health apps not subject to HIPAA. A "breach" includes unauthorized disclosures to third parties, such as ad pixels — [FTC blog](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2024/04/updated-ftc-health-breach-notification-rule-puts-new-provisions-place-protect-users-health-apps); [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/05/30/2024-10855/health-breach-notification-rule); [Covington](https://www.insideprivacy.com/health-privacy/ftc-issues-final-rule-to-expand-scope-of-the-health-breach-notification-rule/)
- BetterHelp paid $7.8M (final order July 2023) and is banned from sharing health data for advertising. It had shared emails, IPs and health questionnaire data with Facebook, Snapchat, Criteo and Pinterest — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/07/ftc-gives-final-approval-order-banning-betterhelp-sharing-sensitive-health-data-advertising?)
- Cerebral (proposed order, April 2024; it also prescribed ADHD medication): a ban on using or disclosing sensitive data for most advertising, and a $10M civil penalty suspended to $2M for inability to pay, for burying data-sharing disclaimers in dense privacy policies — [FTC](https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/proposed-ftc-order-will-prohibit-telehealth-firm-cerebral-using-or-disclosing-sensitive-data); [The Markup](https://themarkup.org/pixel-hunt/2024/04/22/cerebral-to-pay-7-million-fine-and-limit-health-data-use-for-ads-under-federal-order); [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/cerebral-federal-trade-commission-7-million-fine-data-sharing-privacy/713295/)

**Children's data**
- COPPA amendments were published April 22, 2025 and took effect June 23, 2025, with the compliance deadline April 22, 2026. FTC leadership says it is "willing and eager" to enforce. Edtech and push-notification amendments were not adopted — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/22/2025-05904/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule); [Davis Polk](https://www.davispolk.com/insights/client-update/ftc-prioritizes-coppa-enforcement-new-compliance-obligations-take-effect); [Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/2025/05/ftc-publishes-updates-to-coppa-rule)
- India's DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on Nov 13–14, 2025 (sources differ by a day). Most fiduciary obligations, including verifiable parental consent for anyone under 18, security safeguards, breach notice and children's processing restrictions, apply 18 months later, around May 2027. Verification can use details the fiduciary already holds or DigiLocker and authorized tokens. Educational institutions and healthcare providers are exempt for prescribed purposes — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-notified-309928); [Legal500 18-month countdown](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown); [Baker Botts](https://ourtake.bakerbotts.com/post/102lund/india-notifies-final-rules-for-digital-data-protection-act); [Lakshmikumaran & Sridharan](https://lakshmisri.com/insights/articles/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-incremental-tweaks-and-the-way-ahead); [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/dpdp-rules-and-the-future-of-child-data-safety)

**Google Play**
- All apps on any track must complete the Health apps declaration, including apps with no health features, which declare none (mandatory since Aug 31, 2024). Health apps need a privacy policy covering sensitive data — [Play Console Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13996367?hl=en); [Android Developers](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/declare-access)
- Health Connect policy (March 5, 2025) tightened eligibility and justification requirements for health-record data — [Play policy announcement](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15931464)

### Inferences
- Positioning rule: market a "focus, study-habit and distraction-blocking app, designed with ADHD brains in mind" and avoid "treat", "reduce ADHD symptoms" and "diagnose" in the US, EU and India. Run any ADHD symptom tracking or screening questionnaires past regulatory counsel, since they can create "monitoring" or "diagnosis" intended purpose.
- Treat self-reported ADHD status, mood and focus data as health data. Use no ad pixels or SDKs that transmit it: BetterHelp and Cerebral are the template for what not to do, and HBNR treats leaks to ad platforms as breaches. In the EU, self-identified ADHD is likely GDPR Article 9 special-category data needing explicit consent (inference; not sourced in this session).
- Under-18s: in the US, build a 13+ age gate with a separate under-13 COPPA flow, or exclude under-13s. In India, plan DPDP parental consent by about May 2027, or route minors through institutional licences under the education exemption.
- HIPAA generally applies only to covered entities and their business associates. It becomes relevant when selling to clinicians or health plans that exchange PHI with the app (inference from HIPAA scope; not sourced here).

## Moats: data network effects, community, content, cross-device ecosystem, clinical validation, brand

### Takeaway
In consumer focus apps, the durable moats are brand trust (privacy plus ethical billing), habit and identity lock-in (streaks, history), community, and published evidence. Formal clinical validation is a moat only if paired with a reimbursement channel such as DiGA.

### Cited Findings
- one sec's peer-reviewed PNAS study and later research underpin its credibility and press coverage — [one sec research](https://one-sec.app/research/); [Time](https://tollbit.time.com/6240981/social-media-addiction-app/)
- Duolingo's streak base (5M+ users on year-plus streaks) and rising DAU/MAU show habit and identity lock-in as a moat. Duolingo explicitly prioritizes DAU growth over near-term monetization, partly to outpace AI disruption — [Motley Fool](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/08/06/duolingo-duol-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/earnings/2026/duolingo-bets-on-user-growth-to-outpace-ai-disruption/)
- Forest's real-tree mission (more than 2M trees) acts as a brand and purpose moat — [Forest](https://forestapp.cc/)
- Tiimo's neurodivergent team and design-award brand: authenticity plus Apple editorial recognition — [Tiimo](https://www.tiimoapp.com/resource-hub/tiimo-winner-2025-app-store-awards)
- Clinical validation alone did not save Akili or Pear without payer coverage — [Citeline](https://medtech.citeline.com/MT147939/Pear-Bankruptcy-Filing-Highlights-Difficult-Challenges-Facing-Digital-Therapeutics)
- Germany's DiGA demonstrates that validation can become reimbursed revenue for adult ADHD apps such as attexis — [Medice](https://medice.com/en-de/from-the-company/attexis-r-2013-the-first-permanently-listed-digital-health-application-for-adults-with-adhd)

### Inferences
- Over 3–5 years, stack the following: (1) habit data and personal focus history, which raises switching costs; (2) study-group and body-doubling community features; (3) exam-specific content for India's test-prep segment; (4) cross-device blocking across phone, laptop and browser extension, which is hard for single-platform rivals to copy; (5) an outcome study, possibly with a university partner, used for B2B sales and press rather than as a medical claim.
- AI is commoditizing planners and to-do features (Tiimo now markets an "AI planner"), so features alone are a weak moat. Trust and community are stronger ones.

### Gaps
- No quantified evidence found on data network effects in focus apps. The claim is likely weak and unproven.

## Cautionary tales

### Takeaway
The recurring failure modes are leaking health data to advertisers (BetterHelp, Cerebral), hard-to-cancel billing (Noom, Vonage), betting on reimbursement (Akili, Pear), and over-expanding headcount on B2B hopes (Headspace layoffs).

### Cited Findings
- BetterHelp: $7.8M and an advertising ban — [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/07/ftc-gives-final-approval-order-banning-betterhelp-sharing-sensitive-health-data-advertising?); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/02/betterhelp-owes-customers-7-8m-after-ftc-alleges-data-mishandling)
- Cerebral: $7M total (including $5M+ in refunds per coverage) and an advertising-data ban — [Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/cerebral-federal-trade-commission-7-million-fine-data-sharing-privacy/713295/); [FTC](https://ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/proposed-ftc-order-will-prohibit-telehealth-firm-cerebral-using-or-disclosing-sensitive-data)
- Noom: $62M class settlement over cancellation friction — [Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/noom-to-pay-over-60m-to-cancel-automatic-renewal-suit)
- Akili: 3% reimbursed, a pivot, layoffs and a $34M sale. Pear: bankruptcy and a roughly $6M asset sale — [STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2023/09/13/akili-endeavorrx-prescription-business-model-lay-offs/); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/medtech/adhd-game-developer-akili-interactive-be-taken-private-after-being-sold-34m?utm_id=55)
- Headspace: repeated layoffs, 2022–2023 — [MobiHealthNews](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/headspace-health-confirms-second-round-layoffs-less-year)
- Opal's last verified funding was about $4.3M seed (2021, about a $17M valuation per Dealroom). It claims 200k+ users, and revenue data is unreliable, a reminder that the focus/blocker category is often lifestyle-business scale — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/opal-1-1/); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1497465230)

### Inferences
- The founder's checklist from these cases: no third-party ad SDKs touching health signals; easy cancellation with renewal reminders; wellness-only claims unless deliberately pursuing a device pathway; keep burn low, because focus apps can be very profitable at small scale (one sec, Forest) but rarely support venture-scale headcount.

### Gaps
- No found example of a focus/ADHD app facing an FTC or privacy action specifically. The examples are adjacent mental-health and wellness companies.
